Mount Hyjal And Black Temple

The decision system for Phase 3 loot. It does not tell a player what to want. It tells the council who gets a drop first, and what the raid loses by routing it one way instead of another.

draft updated 2026-08-08

Published best-in-slot lists answer “is this item my best in slot?” for one spec at a time. This compendium consumes them rather than competing with them, and answers the question they leave open: when several specs have a real claim on the same drop, which one takes it, and on what grounds.

It carries best in slot as reference, because players arrive expecting specific items and the council should know which expectations a conclusion runs against. Reference is all it is.

The two ways in

A council asks two different questions, so the compendium is entered two ways. When a drop lands the question is who wants it, and Loot By Boss leads to that item’s page and every claimant on it. When a week is being planned the question is what a player still needs, and Loot By Spec leads to what one spec wants, slot by slot. Both read the same shortlist, so the two cannot disagree.

A third way in answers a question neither of those asks. Simulated Throughput reports what each spec measures at three gear anchors, with the spread on every figure, and every figure is a click into the exact set, consumables and buffs behind it. It ranks nothing, and a spec is comparable only with itself.

The tier question gets its own page. Tier measures at what point breaking an old set bonus pays: every subset of a spec’s bonus-carrying slots replaced with its chase pieces, and the Tier 6 two-piece and four-piece thresholds priced on the same baseline.

Start here

Every analytical decision traces back to one document, the Framework. It carries the mission, the full assumption table, the layers a call is built from, and the order in which they win when they disagree. It is where the reasoning behind a call comes from.

What this will answer

Existing sources rank an item against one spec at a time. Five questions sit above that ranking, and they are the ones a loot council has to settle.

Cost of routing

What the raid loses by giving a drop here instead of there.

Token order

Which claimant on a contested token line goes first.

What a set breaks

What a tier piece breaks the night it is equipped.

Flow

In what order items should flow to make the raid strongest fastest.

Equity

Whether loot is reaching people fairly across the phase.

Terms used across this compendium are defined in Conventions, which also carries the worked example: one item, every claimant, and the priority each was given.
