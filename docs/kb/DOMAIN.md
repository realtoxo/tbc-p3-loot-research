# Domain

Facts about The Burning Crusade 2.4.3 and about the outside sources this project consumes. Every one of them has already caused a wrong conclusion here or in a prior repository.

This file holds only what has no other home. The compendium vocabulary lives in [`docs/conventions.md`](../conventions.md), the fact tables document themselves in [`data/facts/PROVENANCE.md`](../../data/facts/PROVENANCE.md), and the reference workbook documents itself in [`data/research/epv-workbook/PROVENANCE.md`](../../data/research/epv-workbook/PROVENANCE.md). None of those is restated here.

## Equipping

Four rules decide whether a spec can hold an item, and none of them is the one people reach for first.

- Armor proficiency is cumulative. A plate class equips mail, leather and cloth, and a leather class equips cloth, so armor type restricts upward only and cloth excludes nobody. <!-- ext: TBC 2.4.3 class armor proficiency, observed 2026-08-08 -->
- The genuine hard locks are weapon proficiency, shields for Druids, class-locked relics, and an explicit class list printed on the item. Nothing else stops a spec equipping a piece. <!-- ext: TBC 2.4.3 equip restrictions, observed 2026-08-08 -->
- An item is never dispositioned on its name. Read the stat line. This project has made that error three times. <!-- unenforced -->
- Some items are owned by a mechanic rather than by a stat line. Wolfshead Helm returns 20 energy on every shift into Cat form, and the EP Workbook ranks it FIRST in the Feral Cat head section at 0.00 EPV with the author's own note, "Yes, its that good." A stat ladder cannot express that, so a higher-scoring head is not automatically the right award for that spec. Check for this class of item before ranking a slot on numbers. <!-- ext: item 8345 read from the 2.4.3 tooltip endpoint, 2026-08-09: item level 45, Leather, 109 armor, 10 spirit, Druid, requires level 40, "When shapeshifting into Cat form the Druid gains 20 energy, when shapeshifting into Bear form the Druid gains 5 rage" -->
- **The parser note that taught everyone to skip that row is in `hit.yaml`.** Its `gear_set_rule_addenda` records the "Yes, its that good." row in the Cat head block as a trap a parser must not read as a section header. That is true and necessary, and it also meant every reader learned to discard the row rather than read it. The Feral Cat capture skipped the workbook's top-ranked head for exactly that reason. A row a parser must ignore is not a row a person should. <!-- ref: data/facts/sim-profiles/hit-capture/feral-cat.yaml -->
- **Wolfshead stacks with Furor, and the pair is the mechanic.** Furor at five ranks returns 40 energy on a shift into Cat form and Wolfshead adds 20, so a powershift returns 60 energy. That is what makes the rotation work, and it is why a helm with no useful stat line outranks one carrying over 300 EPV. <!-- ext: Exa synthesis over TBC Classic feral guides, observed 2026-08-09 -->
- **The exception is a cat that tanks part of an encounter.** The Thunderheart two-piece reduces Mangle cost and improves the rotation for a druid NOT wearing Wolfshead, which is what makes swapping off it affordable when the player expects to take damage for part of a fight. So the rule is strong rather than absolute, and the condition is the role in that encounter. <!-- ext: Icy Veins TBC Classic feral druid DPS gear guide, observed 2026-08-09 -->
- **An Enhancement Shaman runs two weapons of the SAME speed and prefers them slow**, because both hands carry a Windfury imbue. Weapon speed is therefore a property the spec selects on, and a one-hander is not interchangeable with another of equal EPV and different speed. THE WORKBOOK CANNOT SHOW THIS: the Enhancement tab ranks main-hand and off-hand candidates in a single combined One Hand pool, where the Fury tab splits Main Hand from Off Hand, so a fast and a slow mace compete for one place as though a shaman would choose between them rather than want a matched pair. The visible consequence is Syphon of the Nathrezim, 2.8 speed and the slowest one-hander in that section, ranked 25th behind Swiftsteel Bludgeon at 1.5. The two carry the same weapon damage, 100.2 against 100.3, and the whole EPV gap is Swiftsteel's 19 hit and 27 haste against Syphon's 10 extra attack power and an unpriced proc. Read that ranking as a stat comparison, which is what it is, and not as advice about which hand wants which weapon. <!-- ext: guild lead, 10 August 2026 -->
- **That speed rule is UNCORROBORATED by any outside source this project holds, and the deferred simulation has now run AGAINST it.** The three Enhancement recordings captured here, from Darkest, Jambrosay and Defcamp and Melderon, never discuss weapon speed or the imbue choice, and the only Windfury mentions in all 67 transcripts are Defcamp on spell coefficients. It is recorded because the guild lead states it and the guild plays that way, and on 20 August 2026 he ruled it, see `data/judgments/enhancement-weapon-rules.yaml`, so the compendium now enforces it: the Enhancement shortlist, its claimants and its comparison baselines exclude weapons at or under 2.3 speed. The simulation this entry once deferred ran the same day, every matched-speed pair as an enchanted variant of each anchor profile, and at entry and at best in slot the captured mismatched pair measured above every matched pair, 34.6 DPS at best in slot against a standard error near 1; only the tier anchor shows a matched pair winning, and that is Black Temple weapons becoming reachable rather than the rule paying. That is evidence against the rule rather than a refutation of it, because whether the engine models the two-imbue interaction the rule exists for has not been verified against the engine source. The rule stands until the council revisits it, and `data/facts/sim-results.yaml` holds the reading under `what_the_matched_pairs_measure`. <!-- ref: data/facts/weapon-pair-sims.yaml -->
- **A Fist weapon is blunt, and both hunters here dual-wield.** The simulator splits weapons two ways for the sharpening stone and the weightstone: blunt is Fist, Mace and Staff, and sharp is Axe, Dagger, Polearm and Sword. Beast Mastery carries Talon of the Phoenix, a Fist weapon that reads like a blade, so it was given the sharpening stone; only the weightstone, 34340, adds 12 to ranged base damage, which is the half of a hunter that fires. Both hunters also carry Claw of the Phoenix in the off hand, while `consumables.yaml` records a source note that "Hunters do not dual-wield in TBC" and Survival's own off-hand field denies the set. The captured gear governs, the note is kept beside the pick, and neither is decided by hand any longer. <!-- test: tools/extract_consumable_ids.py::verify_weapon_classes -->
- **Wolfshead Helm is not in the simulator database**, so `items.csv` cannot carry it and any set holding it will report one unresolved slot. That is a limit of our table rather than a fact about the item. <!-- unenforced -->

## Tanks

**A Bear's item armor is multiplied and a plate tank's is not, so the two numbers in `set-stats.yaml` are not comparable.** Dire Bear Form multiplies armor contributed by ITEMS, and Thick Hide raises the multiplier further at max rank. Read literally, the table shows the Feral Bear at 5,919 item armor against the Protection Warrior's 15,255 and invites the conclusion that the bear has under 40 percent of the plate tank's armor. Applying the multiplier reverses it: the bear ends above the plate tank. Sources differ on the exact multiplier, roughly 4.7 to 5.0, and on whether bonus armor is multiplied too, so no figure is asserted here. What is asserted is that the raw comparison is wrong. <!-- unenforced -->

**No leather and no mail item in `data/facts/items.csv` carries any defense or dodge rating.** Zero of 153 leather rows and zero of 133 mail rows, against 59 of 158 plate rows carrying defense. Every point of defense a Bear holds therefore comes from the six armor-agnostic slots: neck, back, both rings and both trinkets. This is why ranking a Bear's gear on a damage ladder strips its defense completely, which happened once and produced a set with zero dodge in every slot. The claim is about our curated table rather than about TBC. <!-- ref: data/facts/sim-profiles/hit-capture/feral-bear.yaml -->
