Argues against it
While routing Apostle of Argus he says the best one hander of the phase does not work well for Resto Druid, and that the Crystal Spire is not very good for them. His reason is the effect at the bottom of the tooltip: when a target is below 50 percent health, direct healing heals you for an additional amount, and Lifebloom ticking on the target does not count as a direct heal. For that reason he sends Resto Druid to the staff, Apostle of Argus, which he says they use best of any class, then priest after that, preferring whoever is not getting Crystal Spire.

It depends
Says the item should be player over class, but that the ordering he would otherwise give is Holy Priest, then Resto Shaman, then Holy Paladin, then Resto Druid last. He cites maths done by people in a priest discord showing the proc is most effective for Holy Priests because Circle of Healing gains a lot of added value from it, then Chain Heal, while Holy Paladins only have single target heals. He says he regrets how clear cut it is, and would only reconsider if there were a very large skill gap between the shaman and the priest.
Included for the Circle of Healing mechanism behind the priest-first view.

It depends
Calls it the best in slot healing mace for Resto Druid at 486 healing power, and a fantastic item, but delivers it as bad news: the effect only fires on direct heals, so it works on Regrowth and not on Lifebloom ticks. He concludes the druid will be the absolute last person in the guild to receive it because every other healer has priority. In the meantime he says Light's Fathom Scepter is perfectly adequate at 43 healing power behind, and that Vengeful Gladiator's Salvation closes the gap to 22 behind, which is what he will aim for himself. 486 healing power.
