Speaks well of it
Zatar gives a spec-level order for Cursed Vision: enhancement shaman first because it gains the most stats by a large margin, then hunter or ret, whichever is better, since both can use its hit to ease gearing, then fury, then rogue and arms last because rogues reach their tier helm quickly. He notes rets wait behind many casters for tier, and says there is no shame in giving it to a strong player who simply wants it.
