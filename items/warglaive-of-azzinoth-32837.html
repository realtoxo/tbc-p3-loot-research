It depends
The Fury Warrior segment addresses the warrior versus rogue question directly. He says warriors are superior on cleave and rogues are superior on single target, but that for end game composition you want to stack warriors with warglaives, which he says was the 2021 meta in Sunwell for progression and even more so on farm. He adds that competition among warriors is the drawback, and that securing them may mean playing Prot or Arms instead.
THE WARRIOR CASE, and the only remark that addresses warrior against rogue head on. It is also the second source the attribution rule asks for: fc-003 is a rogue creator praising a rogue weapon. His Sunwell meta claim is his recollection of the 2021 servers and is not restated here as a fact about the Anniversary schedule.
