Measurement

Tokens

A token drops and the council wants the give-out order that maximizes what the item does: each token’s claimants ranked by measured gain, then the same question state by state, because a token’s worth moves with the tier pieces its taker already holds.

draft updated 2026-08-22

Each section is one token: the specs that can redeem it, best measured gain first, then the decision tree. Every ordering here is derived from measurement, and the award itself is the council’s call.

How to read every figure

Gain in a table is the token’s own piece measured alone on that spec’s entry set, against its entry figure. A first piece often reads negative because no set bonus is active yet; the tree below the table is where the thresholds appear. The standing says whether the token piece is in that spec’s best-in-slot set. The plus or minus is one standard error, not a confidence interval.

A tree branch re-prices the same token in a later state: the gain of adding the piece after the pieces named, read as the difference of two measured subset rows from the tier rounds, with the spread combined from both. Nothing is added across slots, because one-slot readings do not survive a hit cap, which the coherence backtest measured; a subset difference needs no adding. The states walked are the spec’s own best pickup order, so a reader follows the road the raid actually takes. Where breaking an old bonus is the cost that makes a branch read low, the break-even is on Tier.

Every piece arrives dressed: the gems and the enchant the exported profile wears on it, or the spec’s standard gem in each socket for a piece outside every profile.
